# Iron Sun sentry concepts

These are approval-stage multi-angle concept mockups, not production 3D models.
Generated views can differ in small mechanical details; final models require a
single consistent mesh, materials, rig or separable moving parts, scale checks,
and rendered Unreal gameplay inspection.

| Role | Player-facing function | Silhouette |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Sentinel | Mid-range pressure with an integrated cannon and telegraphed close strike | Tall armored humanoid, balanced proportions |
| Sprinter | Fragile, evasive melee flanker | Narrow digitigrade runner with long blade arms |
| Brute | Slow, durable siege bruiser with short-range blast | Low, wide hydraulic tank with crusher gauntlets |
| Turret | Stationary area denial with a rotating three-cannon burst and reactor weak point | Ground-anchored, non-humanoid emplacement |

The player approved all four first-arena visual directions. This is a starting
roster, not a cap on future enemy roles. Do not treat concept art as an imported
or playable Unreal asset; model and verify each role separately.
