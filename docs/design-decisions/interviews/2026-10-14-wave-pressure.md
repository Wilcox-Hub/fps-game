## 2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q2

### Question

❓ Q2 — How should we make those opening rounds forgiving?

➡️ I recommend fewer enemies plus lower incoming damage, then ramp both up. The current prototype starts solo with six enemies total, two arriving together, and half the previous opening damage.

Options shown: Fewer enemies and lower damage (recommended) | Fewer enemies; keep current enemy damage

## 2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q3

### Question

❓ Q3 — After the opening, what should drive the rising pressure?

➡️ I recommend enemy numbers, overlapping arrivals and tougher role combinations leading the increase, with health and damage growing more gradually. The 10% health drop will use the previous arena’s final round as its baseline.

Options shown: Numbers and composition lead (recommended) | Health and damage rise just as strongly

### Original answer

Numbers and composition lead (recommended)
