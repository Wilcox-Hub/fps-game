# Waves, arena layout and pressure

Generated from [current.json](../current.json). Read current rules before historical interviews.

## waves-opening — Easy first two rounds of the run

**APPROVED** · Recorded 2026-10-18

Rounds 1–2 of the entire run should feel forgiving through fewer enemies and lower incoming damage. Ramp afterward primarily through numbers, overlapping arrivals and tougher role combinations, with health and damage increasing more gradually.

Sources: [2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q1](../../../docs/design-decisions/interviews/2026-10-14-wave-pressure.md#2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q1), [2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q2](../../../docs/design-decisions/interviews/2026-10-14-wave-pressure.md#2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q2), [2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q3](../../../docs/design-decisions/interviews/2026-10-14-wave-pressure.md#2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q3).

## waves-arena-relief — New arenas ease population and reduce health 10%

**APPROVED** · Recorded 2026-10-18

Later arenas open with fewer enemies. Ordinary role health starts at 90% of the previous arena's final-round baseline, then continues growing from that point. Carry earned difficulty and enemy damage forward; do not reset every arena to the first-run easy opening. Apply co-op scaling using current party size.

Sources: [2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q1](../../../docs/design-decisions/interviews/2026-10-14-wave-pressure.md#2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q1), [2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q3](../../../docs/design-decisions/interviews/2026-10-14-wave-pressure.md#2026-10-14-wave-pressure-q3).

## arenas-layout — Layout follows the approved first-map mockup

**APPROVED** · Recorded 2026-10-18

Keep the planned desert-colosseum layout and visible multiple gate/door arrival locations; enemies walk from coherent entrances rather than popping into the center.
