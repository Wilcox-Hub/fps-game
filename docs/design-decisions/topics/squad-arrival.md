## arrival-lobby — Gather and prepare before the match

**APPROVED** · Recorded 2026-10-18

Launcher Play opens a front end. Host/join a party, choose cosmetic appearance, choose two perks and the shared team-bonus vote in the lobby, ready up, then the leader explicitly starts. Do not launch directly into an active match or timed post-load selection screens.

## arrival-perks — Four available perks; equip two

**APPROVED** · Recorded 2026-10-18

Keep Stable Grip, Rescue Harness, Trauma Kit and Lightweight Rig, available immediately. Each player equips two. Differentiate handling, rescue, healing and movement without superhero powers. Earlier perk lists do not reintroduce removed choices.

## arrival-gate — Squad-controlled colosseum entrance

**APPROVED** · Recorded 2026-10-18

Walk together up broad worn stone/industrial sci-fi steps matching the arena mockups. Protected arsenal/racks sit before the main gate. Collect one free primary and one free secondary without duplicates using the preserved Gale/Phasma/wildcard starter eligibility. No forced preparation countdown; loadout changes clear ready. Open when all active players are equipped/ready; wait for the whole squad to enter, then safely close and begin combat. Later arenas retain gear, use shorter entrances and wait for team readiness; no repeated free starters.
