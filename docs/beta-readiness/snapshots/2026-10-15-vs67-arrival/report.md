# VS67 colosseum arrival and starter arsenal

Approved arrival flow is implemented locally in protocol 67. The lobby carries perk and team-bonus choices into a protected physical staging area; players select a free primary and distinct secondary from the starter racks, take unlimited preparation time, and ready up. The gate opens after the full squad is ready. Later arenas retain weapons and use a shorter readiness entrance. The paid in-arena arsenal keeps known weapon purchases, random-gun rerolls, and upgrades available for shards during combat.
