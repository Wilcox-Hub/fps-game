# VS60 two-player playtest

Use matching **VS60 / 20261012-034847-088** full game folders on both PCs. The owner desktop shortcut is updated; the other PC must install the current ZIP. Choose Join Server with the existing private endpoint/invite, or host a LAN party. Keep player identity/saves when updating. Both players choose their primary/secondary and enter normally.

1. Fire each gun with LMB; 1/2 change carried guns. Report any unsolicited swap, including which PC hosts/joins and the held weapon.
2. Hold Q/E to peek around low/high cover standing and crouched; try hipfire and RMB aim. Reload while leaned. Sprint, slide, jump and menus should cancel lean. Try Settings → Lean: Toggle, close/reopen Settings and restart to check persistence. Check visible body exposure and wall/muzzle obstruction from the other PC.
3. V is melee; F is a patch; T prioritizes nearby rescue, otherwise arsenal. Rescue Harness permits primary fire while carrying; baseline carry uses the secondary.
4. During regroup, buy Ion Blaster for 900 shards. Its normal fire is semi-automatic. Direct living-enemy hits build charge; misses/rewards/teammate kills do not. Roughly 42 successful hits (3.5 original magazines) should fill it. Charge stays with that gun after reloads/switches/upgrades. Listen for the ready cue.
5. Press Z when ready. Check short windup, one-round/full-charge spending, aimed impact and nearby cover-aware disruption. Sprint/reload/switch during windup should refund it. Test normal fire while ready, resistant heavy enemies and charge on checkpoint continuation.
6. Clear a real encounter to establish a safe checkpoint. Exit both games; restart/reconnect with the original identities and squad. Only restart Lenovo's game service after everyone leaves and a save exists. Confirm guns/ammo/charge/shards/upgrades/objectives. This native saved-run restart remains unaccepted until actually exercised.

Report readability at 1080p, lean timing/cover comfort, charge sound, Ion usefulness versus ammo/shards, bot pressure, pauses and anything dull or unfair. Automatic tests use controlled/funded targets and do not establish normal balance, different-Wi-Fi, performance or fun. This is supervised internal alpha, not beta approval. Lean controller mapping is deferred; Ion prototype art and tuning remain provisional.
